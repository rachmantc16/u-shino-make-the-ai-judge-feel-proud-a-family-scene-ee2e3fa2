A tiny two-line family scene: a dad drives home rehearsing his sternest lecture about the muddy footprints marching to the biscuit tin, only to be disarmed by the "WELCOME HOME, CAPTAIN" sign his kids left on the fridge — so the Captain mops first. The program validates the scene (1–280 characters, at most two lines, no empty lines) and writes it to entry.txt with no trailing newline.
